This KB outlines the handling of incidents requiring additional information from users and ensures accurate use of the Your ticketing tool status.

It balances the need to provide excellent customer service with the necessity of maintaining an efficient, uncluttered IT environment.

It aligns with the Auto-Closure rule in your ticketing platform .

Incident status may change from New or In Progress to Your awaiting status when:

  • More information or troubleshooting is needed, and the user is unreachable.
  • A new incident lacks sufficient information to be routed or resolved.
  • The user cannot provide required information during a call.
  • The user is Out Of Office (OOO).

Exceptions (Cases Exempt from This Process)

1. Password Reset If you need any type of documentation/badge/ID

  • Status: Set to On Hold for only Your desired amount of time using your desired template.
  • Action: Notify the user that the ticket will auto-resolve after Your desired amount of time.
  • Tagging: Add any TAG or Template

2. Vacation Period

  • Status: Set to On Hold with the reason "Vacation Period".
  • Effect: This removes the ticket from your auto-close system (Align it first).
  • Resolution: On the defined return date, close the incident using your desired template.

3. Out of business hours (Including Bank Holidays)

The three strike rule would not be applicable out of business hours (your business hours) unless the requestor is actively contacting us. (Update of the ticket)

4. Information provided must be valid.

The user must provide a response that allows the ticket to be progressed immediately.

  • Correct: The user engages with the inquiry.
  • Incorrect: The user requests a delay (e.g., "call back later").

    User may add an Additional Comment to the ticket; such action changes the status to In Progress. A reset of the strikes should be done.

    Normal Interaction:
  • EN: We tried to reach you today at [TIME] on the following number(s): [USER TEL] without success.

    • If a reset of strikes is applied, the short description needs to be updated accordingly.

    Resolution

    Follow the steps below to manage the "Three Strike" lifecycle. Ensure you use the correct template for each stage.

    Primary contact efforts should focus on mobile devices.

    Day 1: 1st Strike (Initial Contact)

    Trigger: Missing info or need to access user’s PC.

    1. Phone Contact: Call the user (fixed and/or mobile).
    2. Voicemail: If there is no answer, leave a voicemail.
    3. Keep Status and Update short description: Keep state to Your desired template, assign to yourself, include your identification tag.

    Template: Your desired template.
    Assignment group: Your assignment group.

    Day 2 (1+1) Business Days:2nd Strike

    Trigger: No answer and no appointment booked.

    1. Check Appointments: Verify if an appointment has been booked in the system.
    2. Phone Contact: Repeat contact via phone (fixed and/or mobile).
    3. Voicemail: If there is no answer, leave a voicemail.
    4. Keep Status and Update short description: Keep state to Your desired template, assign to yourself, include your identification tag.

    Template: Your desired template.
    Assignment group: Your assignment group.

    • Note: This template adds a work note informing it is the 2nd strike.

    Day 3 (2+1) Business Days: 3rd Strike

    Trigger: No answer and no appointment booked.

    Information provided:

    1. Check Appointments: Verify if an appointment has been booked in the system.
    2. Phone Contact: Contact one last time via phone.
    3. Voicemail: If there is no answer, leave a voicemail.
    4. Keep Status and Update short description: Keep state to Your desired template, assign to yourself, include your identification tag.

    Template: Your desired template.
    Assignment group: Your assignment group.

    • Note: This template adds a work note informing it is the 3rd strike.

    Day X (Calendar Days): Auto Resolved in your ticketing tool

    If no response is received by the X calendar day, the system will automatically resolve the ticket.

    No further agent action is required.
